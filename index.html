The figures

How to read it

Every shape is sized by a number. Peak heights are yields, the gap between the derrick and the tanker is the oil spread, the vessel’s draft is debt to GDP, and building widths are basket weights with heights as the year-over-year change.

The connections

Hover anything and the objects it drives stay lit while the rest dims — credit to the 10-year, oil to the fuel depot, fares to the transport shed, the funds rate to the short end, debt to the long bond.
